Settings

Keyboard

Stops wrong white notes when you play fast. At black-key height, a touch just beside a black key plays that black key. Whole top band: white keys start only from their lower part, including where Ga meets Ma and Ni meets Sa. Only between blacks: just the thin strips squeezed between black keys stay silent. Either way, a white note you're holding keeps sounding if your finger slides up.

Upright, the top row is one octave above the bottom row. Sideways, Auto gives one long keyboard of 2.6 octaves (C to G, 19 white keys). Stacked rows use whole octaves.

Jump anywhere: the thin strip at the top is the whole 88-key piano. The bright window is what you see. Tap or drag it to jump straight to any range, at the same key size. You can also swipe sideways on the dark area above the keys to slide the keyboard under your finger.

Slide: ◀ ▶ in the dock move the keyboard one white key lower or higher, keeping the key size; hold to keep sliding. The middle shows the leftmost key, e.g. G3 for a song in Safed 5. A Bluetooth keyboard's ← → still jump a whole octave.

Key size: the − ⌸ + buttons in the dock. + gives fewer, wider keys; − gives more, narrower ones, one white key per tap. Upright and sideways each remember their own size. Picking an octave count above resets it. Press nearer the bottom of a key to play louder.

Rotation lock on? Tap ⟳ at the top to turn the page sideways anyway. Tap again to flip it the other way, and a third time to go back upright.

Harmonium stops

For 🪗 Harmonium · 3 reeds (pulling a stop switches to it). Bass sounds an octave below, Male at the note, Female an octave above. Bass + Male + Female is the classic triple reed. Male 2 is tuned slightly sharp and beats against Male, for the Male-Male-Female kind. Tremolo makes the sound flutter.

Sound

Sound delay

Bluetooth earbuds add 150–300 ms of delay that no web page can remove. The phone speaker or wired earphones feel instant.

Recording


The video is exactly the stage: upright gives a tall video (good for Reels and Status), sideways gives a wide one. Use Share → Save Video to put a take in Photos.

How it works

Every note is built from overtones in your browser, and key n vibrates at 440 × 2(n−69)/12 Hz. The readout on the stage does this sum live.

Try: hold C E G (C major), then slide the middle finger down one key to E♭. One key flips the mood: C minor.
