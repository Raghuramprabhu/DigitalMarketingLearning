Keyboard

Upright, the top row is one octave above the bottom row. Sideways, Auto gives one long keyboard of 2.6 octaves (C to G, 19 white keys). Stacked rows use whole octaves.

Slide: ◀ ▶ in the dock move the keyboard one white key lower or higher, keeping the key size; hold to keep sliding. The middle shows the leftmost key, e.g. G3 for a song in Safed 5. A Bluetooth keyboard's ← → still jump a whole octave.

Key size: the − ⌸ + buttons in the dock. + gives fewer, wider keys; − gives more, narrower ones, one white key per tap. Upright and sideways each remember their own size. Picking an octave count above resets it. Press nearer the bottom of a key to play louder.

Rotation lock on? Tap ⟳ at the top to turn the page sideways anyway. Tap again to flip it the other way, and a third time to go back upright.

Sound

Sound delay

Bluetooth earbuds add 150–300 ms of delay that no web page can remove. The phone speaker or wired earphones feel instant.

Recording

How it works

Every note is built from overtones in your browser, and key n vibrates at 440 × 2(n−69)/12 Hz. The readout on the stage does this sum live.

Try: hold C E G (C major), then slide the middle finger down one key to E♭. One key flips the mood: C minor.
