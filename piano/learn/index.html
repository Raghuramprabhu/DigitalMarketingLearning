Syllabus · first sound → deep theory · two traditions, one keyboard

Two traditions, thousands of kilometres apart,
both named one note "the fifth".

Western music calls it the fifth. Indian music calls it Pa, short for Panchama: "the fifth". That's no coincidence. It's physics, and both traditions found it. This course follows that kind of thread from the very first vibration to melakarta maths, harmony and form. Every idea is shown on a keyboard you can play, heard both ways: Western and Indian, Carnatic and Hindustani.
