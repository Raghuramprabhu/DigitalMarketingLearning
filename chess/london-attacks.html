THE COMMON THREAD

Three ways to break a defence

Different players and different centuries, but in every case the attacker read the defender before striking.

Kamsky · Focus
Strike at the routine moment

The defender is weakest during the move he plays without thinking.

Carlsen · Austerlitz
Hit the ground they just left

Every advance leaves something behind. Look there first.

Ding · Tangail
Close the roads, then strike

Take away counterplay and escape squares. The win then arrives by itself.

🎯

Same front sight, every time. All three losers played a natural move without asking what it allowed: ...Bxd7 / ...Qxc5, ...g5, ...e5. All three winners asked one question before striking: what did that move leave behind?

Your drill for the next 10 rapid games
  1. After every opponent move, ask: what did that piece or pawn just stop guarding?
  2. Before your "obvious" recaptures, spend 5 seconds on: what does this allow? (Shankland's lesson.)
  3. When your knight is on e5 and their f6 knight leaves, check Bxh7+.
