🧩 Before you start
All three of Gukesh's "wow" moments end with the opponent blundering. Is that luck, or a skill?
Tap to think, then reveal
It's a skill, and it has a name in war: attrition. A defender must be right on every move; an attacker only needs one mistake. Gukesh's art is making the defender's job long, tiring and slightly different each move. Watch for the moment the wall breaks in each of his games, then ask: what did he do in the twenty moves before it?

FIRST PRINCIPLES

Two ways to break any defence

Every defence is a set of jobs: this piece guards that square, that pawn blocks this file. A defence breaks when the jobs outnumber the defenders. There are only two ways to make that happen.

Gukesh · add time
Make the jobs last longer

Hastings, Midway, Longewala. Keep the defender busy move after move. Sooner or later one guard is needed in two places, or simply gets tired.

Sindarov · add force
Make the jobs pile up now

Cannae, Julu, Uranus. Bring more force than the defence can handle, right now, even if it costs material.

Both · the overloaded guard
Find the piece with two jobs

Ding's b7 pawn (block the file, guard the knight), Pragg's e7 queen (guard f7, f8 and d7), Nakamura's f3 pawn (guard g4 and e4). Again and again, the game turned on one piece being asked to do two things at once.

Geneva watch-guide. Sindarov arrives rated about 70 points higher after a huge 2026 (Candidates, Olympiad gold, Clutch Chess), and Carlsen and Nepomniachtchi both picked him to win. Gukesh has something Sindarov doesn't: he has already won a world title match.

If you see……it's whose kind of game
Long, level endgames past move 50Gukesh. Remember game 14: his best weapon is the defender's tiredness.

One player low on the clockWhoever has more time. All three Gukesh games here broke open when someone was short of time.
Your drill for the next 10 rapid games
  1. Siege check. In a level position, don't offer a draw. Ask: which enemy piece has two jobs? Make one safe move that adds a third.
  2. Storm check. Before a sacrifice, count attackers and defenders around the king. Sacrifice only if the enemy king can't run away (like Pragg's king after 15.Kd1).
  3. Carlsen check. When you're winning, your opponent's only hope is a trick. Before every move, look at every check they could give back.
