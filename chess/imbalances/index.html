How each chapter works

  • The board fills the screen. Lessons arrive as clouds that move out of the way of the pieces that matter. Tap a cloud to see through it.
  • At key moments it stops and asks you first: find the move on the board, or pick an answer.
  • Halfway through, a war story takes over the screen, then hands you back to the board.
  • Each chapter ends with a fresh position to judge on your own, plus two quick recall questions.
