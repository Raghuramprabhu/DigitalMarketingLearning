THE FIRST PRINCIPLE

Why does everything change when the queens leave?

In the middlegame, the board is a battlefield full of danger. The king hides because one mistake means mate. Pieces are valued by how much damage they can do.

Take most of the pieces off and the danger goes away. Three things that were background noise become the whole game:

♚

The king fights

Mate threats vanish, so the king walks to the centre and becomes one of your strongest pieces.

Mates · opposition · outflanking
♙

Pawns become queens

Every pawn is a potential queen. Races, breakthroughs and passed pawns decide.

Square · breakthrough · Lucena
⏳

Tempo is territory

With few pieces, one move can be the difference between win and draw. Sometimes the best move is the one that gives your opponent the turn.

Zugzwang · mined squares · stalemate
🧭

The question that solves endgames: "Who needs to move, and how many moves does each side need?" Counting replaces guessing. And when counting fails, look for a geometry trick: a diagonal, a detour, a bridge.
