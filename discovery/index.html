Act IV / The unexpected connection

You've seen this
pattern before.

Meena's shop is not the only place where a single symptom hides different causes. Consider four worlds.

🧑‍💻

Software incidents

“Checkout failed” could mean an expired token, a payment outage or a broken form. A failure count is not a root cause.

🏥

Medical diagnosis

A symptom can have multiple explanations. Evidence and appropriate tests matter more than jumping to the first hypothesis.

♟️

Chess

Two positions can look similar but demand different plans because of one hidden structural difference.

🛰️

Engineering control

A sensor measures an outcome. A controller adjusts its actions. But a bad sensor or a misunderstood cause can produce the wrong correction.
